Shared across all three

  • Puffs are darker water and lulls lighter, relative to the course average, with a catspaw texture inside puffs.
  • Wind direction: faint streaks drifting downwind.
  • Current: chunky arrows, thicker and longer where it runs harder. Shallows get a tan tint of the same lightness.
  • Laylines are yellow (#F0E442), dashed when adjusted for current and thicker within 2 lengths.
  • Marks of the current leg are orange with a dashed 3-length zone and a rounding-side arrow. Other marks are grey.
  • Right of way: an orange triangle with "!" on a boat you must keep clear of, a blue chevron on a boat that must keep clear of you.
  • The race-area boundary is a solid line with a hatched band outside it.
  • Liveries use Okabe–Ito colours plus a sail number. Yellow is kept for laylines.

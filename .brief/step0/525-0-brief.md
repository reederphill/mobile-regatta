4. Pack definition in the report: a boat is "in a pack" if >= 2 other boats, racing the gate leg at stage 0, are within 20 m of her at her entry tick (first tick within 80 m of the gate centre and > 3 m above it: `RoundingObservation.entryRange`/`gateAbove`); a race "holds its pack" if >= 1 boat is. 5. With and without current: `current` = `saltings-reach@1` + `classic-oscillating@7`; `without` = `dev-venue@7` + `classic-oscillating@7`. A test shows a boat's `current` is non-zero somewhere in a `with` race and zero throughout a `without` one.
6. The report (text and `--json`) has these columns per group (all / with / without current / by pack size / by offset / by gybes / by tier): races, boats, pack held share, both marks used share, boats on the less-used mark (mean), gate leg completed share, extra metres entry-to-end (mean, median, p90), extra seconds (mean), pack extra metres (sum of the members', mean per race), boat contacts a race, mark touches a race, rule calls a race (total, then by rule), seconds below the line (mean, p90), deepest m (mean, p90), overshoot share (>= 3 s below before the line), still-below share, dirty-air seconds a boat. A test builds a small run and asserts the header and one row per group.
7. Bad usage: `--scenario nope`, and with `--scenario pack` any of `--matrix --thresholds --profile-mix --laps --diagnose` exit 2 (usage); no thresholds, exit 0 on a sailed run.

## Design
- Race: `fleetSize` = pack size n, laps 2, seed s, `BotRaceHarness.windSeed(for:)`. Gate centre `G` = mean of the gate marks (`course.targetPosition(for: course.legs[2])`), `up = course.upwind`, `right = course.right`; hull `L = race.boatClass.hull.length`.
- Bunch centre `C = G + up*pickAbove + right*(offset*L)`. Offsets (hull lengths, + is to the gate's left mark side as the course layout has `right`): -6, 0, +6 (gate is 10 L wide; so over the right mark, the middle, over the left). Positions: n draws in a disc of radius 3.5 L around C (seeded `SplitMix64` from a hash of seed, layout and n; the same stream decides which seat takes which slot, so tier does not follow slot). Layout = (current, n, offset, gybes). `gybes`: `same` = all on the gybe of the first draw; `mixed` = alternate gybes by slot (so a port-gybe boat gives way: rule 10 shows).
- Boat pose: heading from `groundWind(at: C)` and `polar.bestDownwind(tws:)` on her gybe, speed = that optimum's speed, boom side as `place` computes it.
- 36 layouts x 16 seeds x national = 576 races by default. Each races about 60-90 s of sim time; cheap.
- End of a run: every boat has been on a leg > 2 for `RoundingObservation.exitSeconds` (15) s, or 240 s after placement (boat counts as not completed). Observer and tally are built after placement.
- Marks used: the end she rounded, read on her first tick on leg 3, `(position - G).dot(along) < 0 ? 0 : 1` with `along = right mark - left mark` (as `PackProbe`). "Both marks used" = a race with both ends in it.
- Port the measures, not the variant switches, from `git show origin/prototype/525-gate-planner:Packages/RegattaCore/Sources/BotSuite/PackProbe.swift` (neighbour test at entry, dirty air = `boat.shadow < 0.85` ticks while within 80 m, `.prototype/pk.py` `packs()`) and definitions in `.prototype/results/q1.md` header. Skip its mark-room (`entitled`/`passed outside`) columns.

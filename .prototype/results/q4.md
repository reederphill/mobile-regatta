# Q4 cost (PlannerCostTests, host Mac, -O, 16-bot race frozen at the tick with most boats in the 80 m pick range)

Command: `REGATTA_BOT_VARIANT=<v> [env] scripts/heavy.sh swift test --filter PlannerCostTests --package-path Packages/RegattaCore --scratch-path .build/check/RegattaCore -Xswiftc -O`
gatePlanChoice = one planner call (candidates + roll-outs + score), 200 calls per boat in range. Whole tick = `SeatControllers.drive` on the frozen race, 3 phases x 100 (the deciding third of 16 seats; only the 5-6 boats in range plan).
The freeze tick differs per config (the race diverges once the planner steers): 5-6 of 16 boats in range.

Reading: a call costs 1.3-3.6 ms, nearly independent of dt (so a fixed cost dominates: `planContext` copies and extends the wake ribbons for every call, the roll-out steps are the smaller part; not profiled). Budget is "<= 1 ms p99 per tick of the 5 ms": the planner is 3x over at best on the host (the device is slower). Plan-every-3rd divides the mean by 3 but not the worst tick (boats' phases are staggered, one call a tick). Levers not built: skip planning when no boat is within ~40 m and no ribbon is near (then it can only differ from main by model noise), cache the extended wake once per tick, not per boat.
